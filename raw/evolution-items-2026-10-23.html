SOFOS CITY EXCHANGES

Where to get Red, Green, Blue and Yellow Shards

Several evolution items are obtained through the shard exchanges in Sofos City. These are the documented ways to obtain each shard.

Red Shard

Held by wild Dwebble or obtained through the Mining Minigame.

Green Shard

Held by wild Natu or obtained through the Mining Minigame.

Blue Shard

Held by wild Alolan Sandshrew or obtained through the Mining Minigame.

Yellow Shard

Held by wild Sandshrew or obtained through the Mining Minigame.

QUICK ANSWERS

Pokemon Lazarus evolution item questions

Can I buy evolution items in Pokemon Lazarus?

Yes. All evolution items can also be purchased from an Elite Four mart.

Where is Black Augurite in Pokemon Lazarus?

Black Augurite is obtained on Marmaro Island from the girl inside the Fossil House.

Where can I get a Dawn Stone?

The Dawn Stone is available from the Yellow Shard Exchange in Sofos City.

Where can I get a Dusk Stone?

A Dusk Stone can be obtained from an Acrisia Mountains quest or from the Green Shard Exchange in Sofos City.

Where can I get a Water Stone?

A Water Stone can be found at Riverwalk Trail South after gaining access to Surf, or obtained from the Blue Shard Exchange in Sofos City.

Where can I get Razor Fang?

Razor Fang can be found in Wanderer’s Woods after gaining access to Surf and in the Sea of Vulcai.

Where can I get Razor Claw?

Razor Claw can be found in the Fresco Isles and Nyx Trails.

Where can I get Linking Cord?

Linking Cord is available from the Green Shard Exchange in Sofos City.

Where are the Tart, Sweet and Syrupy Apples?

Tart Apple, Sweet Apple and Syrupy Apple are all sold by a street vendor in Sofos City.
